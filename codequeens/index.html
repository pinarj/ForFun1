A Fun Journey Through Computer History! 💻✨

Hey girls! Did you know that computers have been around for more than 80 years? 😮 Let’s go back in time and explore how computers became what they are today!

The 1940s: The Giant Brains 🧠

In the 1940s, computers were huge machines that filled entire rooms! They were used mostly for military purposes. One of the first big computers was called ENIAC, and it was as big as a house! 🏠

The 1960s: The Space Age 🚀

During the 1960s, computers helped humans land on the moon! 🌕 These computers were much smaller than ENIAC, but still very basic compared to today’s laptops. Oh, and they had blinking lights and tons of buttons!

The 1980s: The Personal Computer Boom 💾

By the 1980s, computers started becoming smaller and cheaper, so people could have them at home! This was the time when cool games and colorful screens became popular. Imagine playing games on a computer with only 8 colors! 🎮

Today: Super Smart Computers 🤖

Today, computers are everywhere—on our desks, in our pockets (smartphones!), and even in our cars. They’re super smart and can do amazing things, like recognize faces and talk to us! How cool is that? 😎

And guess what? YOU can be a part of the future of computers by learning how to code! 🚀🌈
